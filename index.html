MindHive Privacy Policy

Your mind stays yours.

MindHive is local-first. Your scores, streaks and training history live on your device, not in a cloud you never asked for. This policy explains, in plain language and full legal detail, what the app processes, why, and the controls you hold over it.

Effective date: 1 January 2026 Jurisdiction: Worldwide Governing laws: GDPR · CCPA · COPPA · COPPA-adjacent stores Applies to: com.mindhive.app (all platforms)

1 · OverviewWHAT THIS POLICY COVERS

MindHive (“the App”, “we”, “us”) is a cognitive-training application. This Privacy Policy describes how the App collects, uses, stores, shares and protects information when you download, install and use it. It applies to all features of the App across every supported device, including free gameplay, optional cloud sync and any in-app purchases.

The App is designed around a local-first principle: core training data is stored on your device and is never required to be shared with us. You are never required to create an account, and no feature of the training experience depends on providing personal information.

By installing or using the App you agree to the practices described here. If you do not agree, do not install or use the App, or uninstall it at any time.

2 · DefinitionsTERMS USED IN THIS POLICY

  • Personal Data — information relating to an identified or identifiable natural person (e.g. a signed-in name or email address).
  • Training Data — your in-app performance records: scores, accuracy, reaction times, session durations, streaks, unlocks and preferences.
  • Device Data — technical information about the device such as OS version, model and app version, used for crash and analytics reporting.
  • Local Storage — on-device storage (a Room/SQLite database and application preferences) that is not transmitted by default.
  • Cloud Sync — the fully optional feature that mirrors Training Data to Firebase Firestore when you sign in with Google.

4 · How We Use Your DataPURPOSES AND LAWFUL BASIS

We use the data described above for the following purposes:

  • Providing the service — to deliver, operate and personalise your training experience (contractual necessity).
  • Syncing across devices — to mirror your Training Data and purchase entitlement when you voluntarily sign in (performance of the service you requested).
  • Improving the product — to analyse anonymised crash and usage data, maintaining stability and performance (legitimate interest).
  • Supporting the free app — to serve advertising and to process your optional Remove Ads purchase (legitimate interest / contract).

Where we rely on legitimate interest you may object at any time by disabling the relevant feature (for example, not enabling Cloud Sync) or by uninstalling the App.

5 · AdvertisingADMOB · REWARDED · INTERSTITIAL · BANNER

The App is demonetised through Google AdMob. When advertising is enabled (i.e. you have not purchased Remove Ads), the following ad formats may appear:

  • Rewarded ads — optional, offered after completing training rounds, granting a one-time in-app boost.
  • Interstitial ads — shown when leaving a game back to the home screen.
  • Banner ads — an adaptive banner on the Stats screen.

Google AdMob and its partners may use your mobile advertising identifier and device data to serve interest-based ads. This processing is governed by Google's advertising policies and their own privacy policy. You can reset or limit ad personalisation through your device's advertising settings, and you can remove advertising entirely by purchasing the Remove Ads upgrade.

During development the App uses Google's public test ad units, which never serve real advertising and never pay out. Production builds are switched to the account ad units before release.

6 · Purchases & SubscriptionsGOOGLE PLAY BILLING

The Remove Ads upgrade is a one-time, non-consumable purchase processed through Google Play Billing. We never see, store or process your card or payment credentials — payment and receipt are handled entirely by Google. We receive only the confirmation that the purchase was completed, which we store locally and (if you are signed in) mirror to your sync data so your entitlement follows you to other devices.

7 · Firebase & Cloud SyncAUTHENTICATION · FIRESTORE · CRASHLYTICS · ANALYTICS

Optional sign-in. If you sign in with your Google account we use Firebase Authentication for the sign-in and Firestore to keep your scores, bests and purchase entitlement in sync across devices.

Crash reports. When a build includes Firebase Crashlytics, anonymised crash diagnostics (device model, OS and app versions, stack traces) may be sent to Google to help us maintain stability.

Analytics. When a build includes Firebase Analytics, anonymised aggregate usage events may be collected to help improve features.

Opt-out. Data is only transmitted under the Firestore/Crashlytics/ Analytics configuration of the specific build you installed. If you never sign in, your Training Data never leaves your device, and you may uninstall at any time.

9 · Children's PrivacyCOPPA COMPLIANCE

The App is not directed at children under 13 years of age, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us personal information, please contact us (section 13) and we will investigate and delete that information promptly. We also comply with store-level requirements for child-directed data handling where applicable.

10 · Data Retention & DeletionHOW LONG WE KEEP EVERYTHING

On-device data is retained for as long as you keep the App installed and is deleted when you clear the App's storage or uninstall it.

Synced data is retained in Firestore while your account remains linked and is removed from our Firebase project when (a) you sign out and delete, or (b) you close your Google account, whichever applies. You may request deletion of synced data at any time via the contact details in section 13.

Deleting the App from one device does not automatically remove data you earlier chose to sync to your Google account. Manage synced data from the App's sync settings or contact us and we will remove it.

11 · Your RightsGDPR & CCPA / CPRA CONTROLS

Depending on your region, you may have the right to:

  • Access — obtain a copy of the Training Data we process about you.
  • Rectify — correct inaccurate or incomplete data.
  • Erase — request deletion of your data where applicable.
  • Portability — export your data in a machine-readable format. The App ships a built-in CSV export from the Stats screen.
  • Object / restrict — limit processing, including disabling Cloud Sync, reminders and ad personalisation at any time.
  • Do not sell / share — we do not sell your personal information; we do not share it except as described in this policy.

To exercise any right, use the in-app controls first (they cover the vast majority of cases), then contact us via section 13. We respond to verified requests within 30 days as required by applicable law.

12 · Data SecurityHOW WE PROTECT YOUR DATA

We apply the following safeguards:

  • Local-first architecture — the default state keeps sensitive Training Data entirely on-device, minimising exposure by design.
  • Encrypted transport — any transmission (sync, ads, purchases) uses TLS over HTTPS to Google's infrastructure.
  • Least-privilege — permissions and data sharing are limited to what a feature genuinely requires.
  • Vetted SDKs — we rely on Google's certified platform services (Play Billing, AdMob, Firebase), which maintain their own security and compliance programmes.

No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure. While we work to protect your information, we cannot guarantee absolute security in the event of an unauthorised breach of third-party infrastructure.
