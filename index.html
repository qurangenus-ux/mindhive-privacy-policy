MindHive

Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 30, 2026

Overview

MindHive (“the App”) is an offline brain-training game for Android. We built it with privacy as a core principle:

The App does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data.

At a glance

  • Fully offline — no internet connection required to play.
  • No ads.
  • No analytics, no tracking, no telemetry.
  • No account, no login, no registration.
  • No third-party SDKs that read your data.

Information We Collect

We do not collect any personal information. The App does not ask for your name, email address, or any identifier, and it does not access your contacts, location, camera, microphone, or files.

Your game progress — scores, reaction times, streaks, and settings — is stored only on your device and never leaves it.

Permissions

The App requests a single permission:

  • Vibrate — used to provide haptic (touch) feedback when you tap during gameplay. No data is read from this; it only produces a vibration on your device.

The App does not request network, storage, or contact permissions.

Optional In-App Purchase (Premium)

MindHive offers a one-time in-app purchase to remove the watermark and unlock extra themes. This purchase is processed entirely by Google Play Billing. We never see or store your payment card number, billing address, or other payment details.

Any payment-related information is handled by Google and is subject to Google’s Privacy Policy.

No Third-Party Data Sharing

Because we collect no data, we have nothing to share. The App contains no advertising SDKs, no analytics SDKs, and no social media login integrations.

Children’s Privacy

The App does not collect any personal information from anyone, including children under the age of 13. Consistent with the Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), since no data is collected, no parental consent is required to use the App.

Data Security

Since all data resides locally on your own device, no data is transmitted over the internet. Your progress remains under your control. If you uninstall the App, any locally stored progress is removed along with it.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page, and the “Effective date” above will be revised accordingly.
